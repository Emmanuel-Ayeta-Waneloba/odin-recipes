Rolex Recipe

A meal so simple but yet so tasty

The Recipe

The Rolex is not a watch in Uganda but the fastest, most nutritious and most affordable fast you can get.

Ingredients

  • Chappati
  • Oil
  • Eggs
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions

Steps Followed

  1. Cook a Chappati
  2. Add oil to pan
  3. Mix egg with tomato and onion
  4. Cook omlette
  5. Add chapati on top and roll together
